About Me

I'm Gilbert Ronoh, a cyber security engineer studying at the University of Eldoret (UOE). My journey started with curiosity about how systems break — and grew into a disciplined practice of offensive security: reconnaissance, enumeration, exploitation, and reporting.

I train daily on TryHackMe, compete in CTF competitions, and build hands-on labs covering web application attacks, network exploitation, privilege escalation, and post-exploitation. I document every engagement as a professional report — because in this field, communication matters as much as the exploit.

My goal: become a full-time penetration tester and bug bounty hunter, contributing to safer software through responsible disclosure.
